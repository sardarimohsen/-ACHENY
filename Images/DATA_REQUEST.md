
# High-Resolution Data on Demand

The images included in this repository or linked via Mendeley may be compressed or resized for efficiency. If your research requires the **original, uncompressed high-resolution images** (e.g., for fine-grained morphological analysis), we can provide them upon request.

### How to Request
To request the full-resolution dataset, please send an email to the corresponding author:

### Support and Sustainability Fee
While the standard version of the ACHENY dataset is freely available for the community, we request a processing fee for access to the **full-resolution, uncompressed imagery**.

**Where does the fee go?**
All funds received from these requests are strictly used for:
* **Project Maintenance:** Covering the costs of high-bandwidth data transfer and secure storage for the high-resolution files.
* **Future Development:** Funding the expansion of the ACHENY project, including the collection of new species, improved botanical labeling, and the development of more advanced deep learning architectures.
* **Open Science:** Supporting the continued availability of the baseline dataset for the global research community.
